Trade unions in post-socialist society: overcoming the state-socialist legacy?

This is a three-year project, from 2004-2007, researching the prospects for trade unions in the CIS countries with research teams in Russia, Belarus, Moldova, Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan, EU partners in the UK, Germany and Slovenia and collaborators from around the world.

Outline of the INTAS Project

INTAS Workshop April 3-5 2006

INTAS Workshop December 14-15 2006

Final Project Seminar Moscow March 15-16 2007
