Russian Research Programme: Working Papers and Reports in English 1992-8

More recent materials on Non-Payment of Wages in Russia, on Poverty in Transition and on Employment Restructuring are listed separately.

The materials listed below are unpublished research materials produced within the framework of the project. In many cases they are not fully referenced, sources are not cited, authors are not indicated and facts may be unchecked. Some of the material may be sensitive. For this reason they are made available to bona fide researchers on a confidential basis for their own use only.

To gain access to these materials please fill in and return the registration form. Completion of this form will indicate your agreement not to quote from any of these materials or to pass them to anyone else without permission. Those registering will be informed by email of updates to the site.

None of this material may be quoted from or cited in any way without express permission. To request such permission send an email to Simon.Clarke@warwick.ac.uk with the Subject field: `permission' indicating precisely what you wish to quote or cite.
