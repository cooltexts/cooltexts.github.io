The development of trade unionism in Russia

This research project was funded from 1999-2002 by the Economic and Social Research Council and by INTAS. It involves local groups affiliated to ISITO in Samara, Moscow, Perm, Kemerovo, Ulyanovsk, Saint Petersburg, Syktyvkar and Yekaterinburg.

A complementary project, monitoring trade union activity in enterprises in Siberia, the Urals and Central Russia, has been funded by the International Centre for Trade Union Solidarity. Reports on the results of the monitoring are available below.

The project proposals and work programmes of the INTAS and ESRC projects and the final reports on the INTAS and ESRC projects can be downloaded, as can a listing of project publications.

Below is a listing of project papers in English and Russian, papers from our seminars in September 1999, September 2000 and July 2001 as well as the final set of papers, regional and case study reports in Russian which are available for downloading, plus the results of a survey of trade union primary group presidents conducted in the spring of 2001. Please note that these materials are only available to registered users of the site.

The fieldwork was completed in March 2001. The final regional reports were circulated among the groups for comment in April 2001 and then revised by October 2001. Federal reports are incomplete, but final versions will be ready by the end of 2001.

There is also a very substantial database of interview materials from five regions, which have been coded in Atlas-ti, which can be downloaded by registered users of this site.

A book reporting some of the results of the project, Sarah Ashwin and Simon Clarke: Russian Trade Unions and Industrial Relations in Transition, is in press with Palgrave, to be published in 2002. You can download the book to inspect here, but for copyright reasons you may not cite or print this version. Please order the book for your library, or even buy it for yourself.
