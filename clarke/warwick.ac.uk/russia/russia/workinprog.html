Working Paper on wage differentiation

Simon Clarke: The Economics and Sociology of Wage Differentiation: the Russian case

This paper argues that patterns of wage differentiation are inconsistent with competitive labour market theory, but are consistent with sociological and industrial relations theories of wages. Individual data consistently shows that much the largest component of wage differentiation in Russia is between firms in the same occupation in the same labour market. This is confirmed by the limited amount of firm-level wage data that I have managed to get hold of. If you have better data, I would love to hear from you!

The paper needs a lot more work, when I get time, so any comments (and access to other data) would be very welcome!

Market and Institutional Determinants of Wage Inequality in Russia is a shorter and neater version of the paper.
