Innovation in post-Soviet industry

This project on innovation in post-Soviet industry, from September 1998 to October 2001, was funded by the EU's INCO-COPERNICUS programme. The project was conducted on the basis of collaboration between ISITO, the Centre for Labour Market Studies (TsIRT) of the Institute of Economics, Russian Academy of Sciences and teams from Minsk, Belarus and Tbilisi, Georgia. The original application and work programme of the project can be downloaded.

All of the project materials can be downloaded from this site. All of the research materials and most of the papers and publications are in Russian. English-language materials available are a report on the first stage of the project (Veronika Kabalina and Simon Clarke: Innovation in Post-Soviet Industrial Enterprises); a rough English summary translation of papers presented to the first project seminar; the final report to the European Commission on the project as a whole; and the papers presented to the final dissemination seminar.

The first year of the project involved 143 short case studies of innovating enterprises. On the basis of the case studies, each regional group prepared a regional report, and participants in the project prepared thematic papers presented at a project seminars in September 1999. On the basis of these reports and discussions a final report on the first stage of the project was prepared.

The second stage of the project involved a smaller number of longitudinal case studies of the innovation process, monitoring innovations from conception to execution. Regional reports were also prepared by each group on the second stage of the fieldwork and thematic papers presented at project seminars in September 2000, July 2001 and a final dissemination seminar in October 2001.

Two books of essays have been published and a third is in press. All three volumes are edited by Veronika Kabalina:
