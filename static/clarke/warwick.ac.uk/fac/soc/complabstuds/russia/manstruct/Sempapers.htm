Management structures, employment relations and class formation in Russia

Papers presented at project seminars

We have held three week-long seminars to discuss the findings of the project, with the participation of all the active researchers. In each case a list of proposed topics for analytical papers was circulated and researchers submitted abstracts of their proposed papers for approval, to ensure conformity with the objectives of the project and to avoid excessive duplication. In some cases two or more people prepared papers on the same theme.

The first seminar, in April 2004, was devoted to the process of comparative analysis. Participants presented initial hypotheses for discussion among all the researchers, who could bring their different perspectives and experience of their own case studies to bear. On the basis of these discussions the researchers then conducted intensive analytical work on their chosen themes to prepare papers for presentation at the seminars in April and December 2005. At these seminars the papers were initially discussed in detail over four days in "expert groups", consisting of papers on related themes, and the outcome of these discussions was then reported and further discussed at meetings of all the researchers over the final two days of the seminar.
