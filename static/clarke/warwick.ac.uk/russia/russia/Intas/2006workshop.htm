Best practice of primary trade union organisations in the CIS countries: comparative experiences

INTAS Project Second Annual Workshop, 3-5 April 2006

Sputnik Hotel, 38 Leninsky prospect, Moscow

Please inform Vadim Borisov (icftumos@cq.ru) if you are intending to participate in the seminar and inform him of your accommodation requirements.

To reach the Sputnik Hotel, take the Metro to Leninsky Prospekt and then walk (or take a trolleybus) about 400 metres south along Leninsky Prospekt and you will see the hotel on your right. Alternatively take a trolleybus from Oktyabrskaya metro down Leninsky Prospekt and the hotel is on the right about 400 metres after you pass Gagarin's phallo-erotic statue.

Programme:

The seminar will start at 10 am each morning in the Press Hall of Sputnik Hotel. The first two days will be devoted to presentations of the findings of each research team regarding the best practice of primary trade union organisations in each of the target countries.

On the morning of April 5th we will discuss the work programme for the final year of the project.

Case study reports and presentations of the research teams and additional materials will be posted here as they are received.
